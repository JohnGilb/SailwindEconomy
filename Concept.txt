In the base Sailwind game, trade is quite simple on a number of dimensions:

1) Any good can be sold to any port
2) When purchasing a good, you can see the price it will likely sell for at nearly every port in the game
3) The player's ability to move goods far surpasses the throughput of the NPCs to move goods

This means that the player's decision making is quite limited:
1) When you arrive at a port, you can check every good it has available, and also check what they will sell for.
2) You pick the best one, load your ship, and go to the port where it will sell for the most.

I'd like to change up these assumptions and create a more interesting and dynamic trade system.

New Trade Principles:
Unique Locations: Ports have specific "types" and "development levels" that influence what goods they consume and produce
Fog of Trade: Players have no explicit knowledge of how much a good will be valued until they visit the location (or gain information about that location via some other means)
The World Spins On: NPC traders represent the bulk of the economy, with the player having to work harder to find an edge.
